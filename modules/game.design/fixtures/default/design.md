# Noodle Rush

Humanoid riders swing floppy noodles through a wild daytime race packed with loops, twists and hover sections. Stay close, shove rivals sideways and chase the lead through the final plunge.

## Track

A wavy circuit, 960 m around, 18 m wide, hills of 3.0 m. 3 laps.

- 2 loops (radius 16 m)
- 2 corkscrew twists
- a 22 m climb and plunge
- corners banked up to 77 degrees
- glowing hover sections on loops, twists and steep banks (karts lift off and get a boost)

Wide bends invite close fights before two loops and two twists turn the pack upside down. Glowing hover sections boost karts through the tilted corners, while a long climb ends in a steep plunge.

## Karts

6 karts: Zibbo, Vemli, Pogrin, Dazzu, Wiblet, Tufflo. Top speed 115 km/h, arcade grip.

Rounded karts have chunky bumpers and open seats that keep each humanoid rider and noodle swing easy to see.

## Riders and combat

Each kart carries a humanoid rider with a noodle. A swing reaches 5.0 m, then needs 1.4 s to recover. A hit knocks the victim sideways and cuts its speed to 65%, with a burst of colour.

Swing your noodle at rivals within 5 metres; each swing has a 1.4 second cooldown. A hit shoves the rival sideways and cuts their speed to 65 percent of its current value. Fight for space through the bends and use hover sections to surge ahead.

Humanoid riders have clear heads, torsos, arms and legs, with padded suits matching their kart colors and oversized floppy noodles.

## Winning

Be the first rider to finish three laps.
